In 2025 the team's graph transformer was selected by 5-fold cross-validation over 15-second chunks, so chunks of one recording sat in both training and validation. Validation accuracy climbed to 99%. The re-evaluation splits every fold over subjects.

Three ways to score it

The bars use the metric each source reports: chunk accuracy for the 2025 runs, subject-level balanced accuracy for the re-evaluation (whisker: 95% bootstrap CI over subjects). The re-evaluation did not re-run the graph transformer; its bar is the best pre-specified model, a soft vote of three feature models.

Each dot is one subject's saved out-of-fold P(AD), P(CN), P(FTD), from a model that never saw that subject; its corner region is the predicted class.

The observed value is the mean of the 10 repeats, while each permutation runs one 5-fold CV, whose null is wider, so these p-values are conservative (as the repository's README notes).
