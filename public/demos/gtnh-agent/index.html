gtnh-agent · live demo

One safety-checked agent cycle, in your browser

The repo's own TypeScript agent loop runs here against its mock Minecraft world: observe, check the state, decide, validate, execute, verify, log to SQLite. No model and no server: the rule-based router decides and the mock planner serves its fixture plans.
