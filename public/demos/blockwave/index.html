Its policy right now

    Empowerment reward per placement

    What it was trained to maximize: how many distinct boards its next two pieces can still reach, minus the same count on an empty board. Zero while the board has room, negative as options run out. A top-out is charged about −607 (marked red): zero control for every placement the dead game would have had.

    It acts on two 24×10 grids (locked cells, active piece) and the next two pieces. Score, lines and level are in the picture for you; none of them reach the agent.

    The pixel agent sees only an 88×88 grey crop of the screen with the score panel blanked, four frames at a time. Its policy is a 1.7M-parameter conv net; its weights are another 6.8 MB.
