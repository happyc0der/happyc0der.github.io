mldsa-auth post-quantum handshake, both sides in this tab

C11 → WebAssembly · runs in your browser

Two parties authenticate with ML-DSA-65 and agree a key from X25519 and ML-KEM-768.

ClientHelloServerHello + sig_BClientAuth + sig_AChaCha20-Poly1305 records

The repo's own handshake and record-layer C code, compiled to WebAssembly, plays Device A and Server B. Run it, break it on purpose, and time it on your machine.

Downloads about 0.17 MB of WebAssembly, then generates three ML-DSA-65 keypairs.

Native reference, from the repo's README: 0.45 ms median handshake and 736 MiB/s record sealing at 64 KiB, in process on an Apple M4 Pro (clang -O3, liboqs's NEON code). The numbers here come from a different build on your machine: liboqs's portable C compiled to WebAssembly, with no SIMD, run by your browser.
