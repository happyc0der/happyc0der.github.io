The full lab needs a window about 700 pixels wide or more: its panels are laid out for a desktop. On this screen, the Atlas engine demo runs the same simulation with a page built for touch.

right-drag pan · wheel zoom · click recolour a cell · Space pause · . step · 1–4 speed · U unbounded · M map mode · R reset view · F5/F9 save/load · Esc quit. Panels move by their title bars and fold by their arrows; the lab places them for a 1280×720 window. Touch: tap recolours, two fingers pan and pinch.

What runs: Atlas’s apps/lab (main.cpp unchanged) and every engine module it links, from atlas-engine 0232309, compiled with Emscripten 6.0.9: the kernel and the lab world, SDL3 windowing and input, the cell renderer and id-pass picking, the Dear ImGui overlay, audio and the in-process lockstep loopback. Replaced for the web: the SDL_GPU backend of engine/rhi is a WebGPU backend written for this demo; the HLSL shaders are translated by glslang (as Atlas cooks them) and Tint to WGSL; the overlay draws with ImGui’s WebGPU renderer instead of its SDL_GPU one; ENet and WAMR are null stand-ins, so --listen, --connect and --mod fail cleanly; one thread (--workers 0), because GitHub Pages cannot enable SharedArrayBuffer. Times in the overlay are browser timings, from a performance.now() rounded to 100 µs steps in Chrome and Edge and more coarsely elsewhere.
